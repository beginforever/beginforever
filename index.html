Questions

The things people actually want to know

What makes Begin Forever different from other matrimony platforms?+
Every profile is verified against a government-issued ID and manually approved — not by an algorithm. No fake profiles. We serve all faiths equally, and women's safety was a core design principle from day one, not an afterthought.
Which IDs are accepted for verification?+
Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, or Passport. Your ID is encrypted, stored separately from your profile data, and never shown to other members.
Which faiths and denominations are supported?+
All of them — Hindu, Muslim, Christian (Catholic, Protestant, Pentecostal, Orthodox, Mar Thoma, and more), Sikh, Jain, Buddhist. Built for every faith in India equally from the beginning.
Is Begin Forever free to join?+
Yes. Creating and verifying your profile is completely free. Founding members (first 300) get 1 week free premium on any plan — no credit card required.
Can my family be involved in my profile?+
Yes. You can register for a son, daughter, sibling, or relative. Begin Forever was designed for how Indian families actually approach the search for a life partner — together.
